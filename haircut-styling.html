Professional Advice

Haircut Care & Longevity Tips

Simple daily habits to keep your fresh cut looking sharp between salon visits.

01

Maintain Regular Trim Cycles

Schedule a quick dusting or perimeter trim every 6 to 8 weeks to stop split ends from traveling up the hair shaft.

02

Use Thermal Heat Protection

Always mist a heat protectant spray prior to using blow dryers, curling wands, or flat irons to prevent brittle ends.

03

Choose Weightless Styling Products

Avoid heavy silicones and waxes that weigh down layered textures. Opt for lightweight mousses, sea salt sprays, or dry texture powders.
