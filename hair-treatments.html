Smoothening vs Keratin: Which Is Right For You?

Choosing between smoothening and keratin depends on your daily styling aspirations. While Keratin Treatment infuses pure proteins to coat and seal the cuticle without permanently altering internal bonds, Hair Smoothening re-aligns hair structure for sleek, straight manageability.

Both services substantially reduce daily blow-drying time, eliminate humidity puffiness, and leave strands feeling soft, fluid, and brilliantly reflective.

Treatment Maintenance Tips

Protect your investment and maintain softness long after your salon appointment.

01

Always Use Sulfate-Free Shampoos

Sodium Lauryl Sulfate strips keratin and natural oils rapidly. A gentle sulfate-free cleanser preserves moisture and smoothing results.

02

Apply a Weekly Deep Mask

Incorporate a rich hydrating conditioning mask for 10 minutes once weekly in place of standard daily conditioner.

03

Sleep on Mulberry Silk or Satin

Cotton pillowcases create coarse friction that induces frizz and cuticle roughness. Silk surfaces protect smoothness overnight.
