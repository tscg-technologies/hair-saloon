Colour Longevity

Hair Colour Maintenance Advice

Simple post-care practices to prevent premature fading and maintain vibrant lustre.

01

Wait 48 Hours Before Shampooing

Allow the newly deposited pigments to fully stabilize and the cuticle layer to close before your first home wash.

02

Use Sulfate-Free Colour Care

Harsh foaming surfactants strip color molecules rapidly. Always wash with gentle, sulfate-free, color-safe shampoos.

03

Rinse with Lukewarm or Cool Water

Hot water forces hair cuticles wide open, washing away precious color tones. Cool rinses seal the cuticle for glossy reflection.
